## Cross-cutting research candidates

| Module | Paid question to validate | Minimum evidence/test | Dependency / constraint |
|---|---|---|---|
| `privacy-audit` | Will device owners, MSPs or procurement buyers pay for a reproducible connected-device telemetry report? | Explicit device/network authorization; baseline configuration; DNS and traffic capture; endpoint inventory; repeatable control comparison | Packet payload encryption can prevent semantic classification; measured destination/volume is not proof of listening/recording |

| `reliability-control` | Can measured agent regressions be audited under a procurement-relevant acceptance contract? | Source-bound tasks, independent verifier, failures with reproducible traces | Prior Director research rejects generic standalone agent-security audit as a default startup thesis |

## Research tests before implementation

1. **Falsify buyer economics:** identify prior expenditures, direct reachability, existing substitutes, measurable pain, vendor saturation, support/liability risk and procurement barriers. Technical novelty alone is insufficient.
2. **Evaluate evidence:** record exact device model/firmware, capture method, request/grant, timestamps, known destinations and uncertainty. Separate *observed telemetry* from *inferred purpose*. Do not assert universal behavior across brands.
3. **Avoid broad collection:** favor metadata and synthetic test events; omit personal audio/video content absent a specific approved necessity and lawful basis.
4. **Define robust safety/rights:** no intrusive scans without authorization, no credential acquisition, no surreptitious capture, no regulated-client claims, and no automatic consumer monitoring deployment.
5. **Quantify value only when real observations exist:** verified pilot revenue, time saved, false-positive rate, ongoing data/license cost, maintenance and complaint/incident exposure.

## Non-product market theses

Power generation/grid ownership, professional underwriting or clinical accountability, wet labs/physical testing throughput, consumer distribution moats and premium in-person experiences can influence **customer selection**, but the supplied material does not establish affordable, licensed, single-engineer AXIOM implementations. Do not create capital-intensive projects from these themes without separate buyer/assets evidence.
