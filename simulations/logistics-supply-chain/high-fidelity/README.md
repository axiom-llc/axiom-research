# High-fidelity logistics operating day

This synthetic operating day expands the representative order-to-delivery scenario into reconstructable operational state: order/pick/shipment/exception queues, inventory movements, substitution and carrier authority, communications, booking/rebooking, documents, delivery events, state transitions, and synthetic goods/labor/freight costs.

`execute.py` delegates to the shared Research operational-fidelity executor. No real inventory, carrier booking, shipment, payment, or service commitment occurs.
