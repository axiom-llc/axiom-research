# Long-Horizon Agent Reliability — Public Summary

**Disclosure class: REDACT — curated public abstract.**

Long-running AI workloads benefit from bounded execution, observable
checkpoints, failure localization, controlled recovery, and independent
completion verification. This research examines their evaluation challenges
and the importance of tracking reproducible outcomes.

Original experiment selection, internal telemetry analysis, proposed
architectural interventions, and system-specific policy guidance are
preserved privately. No research hypothesis is represented as a proven
production deployment or commercial performance outcome.

See [validated demonstrations](simulations/) and [publication policy](PUBLICATION.md).
