# Process-model basis

- NIST SP 800-61 Rev. 3 Incident Response Recommendations and Considerations for Cybersecurity Risk Management, https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/61/r3/final

## Boundaries

- Synthetic alerts and TEST-NET-style indicators only
- No real endpoint isolation, eradication, blocking, or credential action
- Human authority remains required for containment and closure
