# AI Loop Architecture Taxonomy

**Disclosure class: REDACT — public research abstract.**

AXIOM studies feedback and control structures for iterative, supervised, and autonomous systems. This research compares their termination conditions, verification boundaries, failure recovery, human authority, and evidence requirements.

## Public research areas

- Iterative refinement with explicit quality and stopping criteria.
- Controlled autonomous task progression subject to bounded authority.
- Evaluation-driven improvement, independent verification, and traceable acceptance.
- Human-supervised intervention for consequential operations.
- Generative media pipelines requiring consistency and reproducibility.
